# 🌊 The North Louisiana Pulse

> **Real reporting. Real resources. Real help. Serving Northeast Louisiana.**

Independent accountability journalism, community resources, and financial aid consulting — built for and by the North Louisiana community.

---

## 📌 About

The North Louisiana Pulse is a community-first platform serving Northeast Louisiana with three core offerings:

- **Accountability Journalism** — Documented, source-backed reporting on local government, economic development, and public institutions. Free to read, always.
- **Community Resource Hub** — A one-stop guide to financial assistance, healthcare, housing, food programs, and government services across the region.
- **Financial Aid Consulting** — Personal, one-on-one guidance navigating FAFSA, Pell Grants, Parent PLUS Loans, and university financial aid offices.

We believe communities thrive when they have access to honest information, real accountability, and practical resources. Transparency is not a threat to progress — it strengthens it.
